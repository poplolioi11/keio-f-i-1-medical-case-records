# 醫案（慶應義塾大學 F@イ@1，1858年記事） / Ian [Medical Case Records]: Transcription Dataset

《醫案》（Ian）是慶應義塾大學富士川文庫 F@イ@1 五丁漢文抄本，開卷記安政戊午（1858）秋的病候，並記個別就診者的診察、處置與追診，另有醫論、朱筆點讀及署安政六年的批記。可用於研究幕末日本漢文醫案的記錄方式與批閱。

Ian (Medical Case Records), Keio Fujikawa Collection F@イ@1, is a five-leaf Literary Chinese manuscript. Opening with observations dated to autumn 1858, it records individual visits and follow-up, followed by medical discussion and vermilion editorial notes dated 1859.

9幅含題簽、正文、黑批與朱墨。頁面、區域和文字層隨原件定位。

The 9 scans cover the covers, main text, black marginalia and vermilion annotations, linked by page and textual region.

## 文字與覆核 / Text and review

AI 輔助初錄。保留原件缺損、疑讀及全冊影像定位。未做人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查或準確率量測。正文、黑墨眉批、朱墨與書庫標記分層。可定位的朱筆事件保存在 JSON；疑字與原件缺損另記。

AI-assisted initial transcription with source references and marked uncertainties. Human full-text double proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement were not performed. Main text, black marginalia, red annotations and library markings are distinguished. JSON records the positions of identifiable vermilion marks and the uncertainties.

TXT 依幅、面與欄提供閱讀定位。JSON 和 TEI 採同一固定取文。「□」標難辨字，缺幅或不確定缺字處由頁面說明及疑義資料標示。完整提示、私有覆核過程與專用程式未隨附。AI 實際模型路由與用量未知。
